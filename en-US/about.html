Our Institutional Manifesto

We believe human dignity is not a concession, but an inalienable human right. We were born out of active indignation towards structural inequality that marginalizes communities in the Federal District Suburbs.

We work alongside families, not merely for them. Our commitment is to provide methodologies, educational scaffolding, and economic avenues so every individual becomes the builder of their own future.

Milestones of Impact

  • 2020: Established in Valparaíso de Goiás as a grassroots response to nutritional insecurity during the global crisis.
  • 2022: Inauguration of the tutoring educational hub and rollout of our first community agroecological gardens.
  • 2024: Implementation of certified technical training programs in industrial baking, textiles, and computer science.
  • 2026: Over 12,480 people supported, independent external audit confirming 91.2% social efficiency, and regional expansion.
